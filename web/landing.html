Who we are

Technology that solves problems that matter.

XP Technology is an electronics technology company focused on the design and development of IoT hardware, intelligent electronic systems and technology education. We combine engineering, research and practical learning to create solutions for African homes, businesses and industries.

Our approach brings hardware, software and connected services together — with a long-term commitment to local manufacturing, accessible innovation and skills development.
