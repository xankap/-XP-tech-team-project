Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 November 2026

This policy explains how XP Tech Ltd ("XP Tech", "we", "us") collects, uses and protects your personal data when you use the XP Tech gas meter app and website at xptechltd.com (together, the "Service"). XP Tech Ltd is based in Enugu, Nigeria. If you have any question, contact us at support@xptechltd.com.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, phone number and, for retailer accounts, your business name.
  • Google account basic profile: if you choose Google Sign-In, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google. We use these only to sign you in and set up your account.
  • Location: your location (latitude and longitude), when you allow it, so we can show gas retailers near you. Retailers also provide their business location so customers can find them.
  • Gas meter data: readings from your XP gas meter (such as gas level, cylinder weight, battery level and leak status), the time of each reading and your usage history.
  • Device identifiers: the Bluetooth identifier (MAC address) of the gas meter you pair, so we can link the meter to your account.
  • Technical data: basic logs our servers keep to run and secure the Service (for example IP address, time of request and error details).

2. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account, sign you in and keep you signed in.
  • To verify your phone number or email address with one-time codes or links.
  • To show your gas level, usage history and alerts, and to back up meter readings you collect offline.
  • To show nearby gas retailers and let retailers manage their listings.
  • To keep the Service working, fix problems, prevent abuse and meet our legal obligations.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell, rent or trade your personal data to anyone.

3. Where your data is stored and who processes it

  • Supabase hosts our database in its EU region.
  • Vercel hosts our website and backend API.
  • Sendchamp delivers our SMS and email messages (such as verification codes).
  • Google provides Google Sign-In, if you choose to use it.

These providers process data only on our behalf, to provide their services to us. Because some of them operate outside Nigeria, your data may be transferred abroad. We rely on providers that apply appropriate safeguards, as required by the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. We may also disclose data if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of people or the Service.

4. Google user data

XP Tech's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We request only your basic profile (name, email address and profile picture) and use it only to sign you in and run your account. We do not use Google user data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not let people read it except where you ask us to (for example in a support request), where it is needed for security or to comply with the law.

5. How long we keep your data

We keep your account data while your account is active. Meter readings and usage history are kept while your account is active so you can see your history. One-time verification codes and expired login sessions are deleted or expire automatically after a short time. When your account is deleted, we delete or anonymise your personal data within 30 days, unless the law requires us to keep some of it for longer. On your phone, the app keeps recent readings so it can work offline until they are backed up.

6. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, access controls on our database and short-lived login tokens. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and will tell you and the authorities about a data breach where the law requires it.

7. Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and other laws that may apply to you, you can ask to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy of it;
  • correct data that is wrong or incomplete;
  • delete your account and personal data;
  • object to or restrict some uses of your data, or withdraw consent you gave (for example to location access, which you can also turn off in your device settings).

To delete your account or use any of these rights, email support@xptechltd.com from the email address on your account (or tell us the phone number on it). We will reply within 30 days. You can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

8. Children

The Service is not meant for children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we do, we will change the effective date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app or by email.
